Net Metering: NEM 3.0

California's NEM 3.0 (Net Billing Tariff), effective April 2023, reduced export compensation to roughly 25% of retail rates. This roughly doubled payback periods versus NEM 2.0. Solar + battery combinations are now more attractive because excess generation is worth less than self-consumption.

Key Incentives in California

  • Self-Generation Incentive Program (SGIP) — rebates for battery storage
  • Property Tax Exclusion — solar systems are not added to your property tax assessment
  • DAC-SASH — up to $8,000 for income-qualified households in disadvantaged communities

Major Utilities

PG&E, Southern California Edison (SCE), SDG&E. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 31.35¢/kWh and ~6,500 kWh of annual usage, California homeowners typically see payback periods between 7.9 years (in Santa Barbara County) and 11.0 years (in Humboldt County). Installation cost of $2.85/W is above the national median of ~$2.70/W.
