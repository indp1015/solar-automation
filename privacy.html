Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

Solar Data Hub ("we", "us", "our") operates solar.dataindp.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration. We do not collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or payment information. The Site does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

3. Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact us via the email address listed on the About page, we will receive the contents of your message and your email address. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and do not add it to any mailing list.

4. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data — including IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamps — for security, diagnostics, and aggregate traffic analysis. These logs are retained for a limited period and are not linked to any personally identifying information.

5. Cookies, Advertising, and Analytics

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality, anonymous usage analytics, and display advertising.

Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the right to consent to or refuse cookies used for advertising. If you are a California resident, you have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information under the CCPA. We do not sell personal information.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from using the core features of the Site. Disabling advertising cookies may result in less relevant ads but does not affect your access to content.

6. Third-Party Links and Data Sources

The Site contains links to third-party websites and references public data sources, including:

  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) — electricity rates
  • PVGIS (European Commission JRC) / NREL NSRDB — solar radiation data
  • DSIRE — incentive information
  • Google AdSense — display advertising

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell any)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we collect minimal data, most such requests will result in us confirming that we hold no personal information about you. To exercise any right, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
