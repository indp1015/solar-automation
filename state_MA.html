Net Metering: SMART + 1:1 Net Metering

Massachusetts combines 1:1 net metering (for systems under 10 kW on investor-owned utilities) with the SMART program, which pays a fixed $/kWh production incentive for 10 years. This makes MA one of the best solar markets in the country despite moderate sun.

Key Incentives in Massachusetts

  • SMART Program — fixed $/kWh production incentive for 10 years
  • State Tax Credit — 15% of system cost up to $1,000
  • Property Tax Exemption — 20-year exemption on solar value

Major Utilities

Eversource, National Grid, Unitil. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 30.41¢/kWh and ~6,500 kWh of annual usage, Massachusetts homeowners typically see payback periods between 5.8 years (in Nantucket County) and 6.6 years (in Berkshire County). Installation cost of $3.30/W is above the national median of ~$2.70/W.
