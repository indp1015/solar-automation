About Louisiana Solar

At an average residential rate of 12.52¢/kWh, Louisiana homeowners typically see solar payback periods ranging from 14.1 to 15.0 years for a 6kW system after the 30% federal ITC.

Net metering policies, utility buyback programs, and local installation costs vary widely within the state. Check your utility's current rules before signing a solar contract — small policy differences can extend payback by 2–4 years.

Sources: EIA Form 861 (2025), PVGIS v5.2 (NREL/NSRDB).
