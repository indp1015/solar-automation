About Connecticut Solar

At an average residential rate of 29.31¢/kWh, Connecticut homeowners typically see solar payback periods ranging from 6.4 to 6.5 years for a 6kW system after the 30% federal ITC.

Net metering policies, utility buyback programs, and local installation costs vary widely within the state. Check your utility's current rules before signing a solar contract — small policy differences can extend payback by 2–4 years.
