Net Metering: Utility-specific net metering

Arizona has no statewide net metering mandate. APS and SRP offer export compensation at rates well below retail (typically 6–10¢/kWh vs ~14¢ retail). Self-consumption with battery storage delivers the best ROI in most territories.

Key Incentives in Arizona

  • State Tax Credit — 25% of system cost up to $1,000
  • Property Tax Exemption — solar systems exempt from property tax
  • Sales Tax Exemption — solar equipment exempt from state sales tax

Major Utilities

Arizona Public Service (APS), Salt River Project (SRP), Tucson Electric Power. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 15.26¢/kWh and ~12,500 kWh of annual usage, Arizona homeowners typically see payback periods between 8.6 years (in Cochise County) and 9.1 years (in Coconino County). Installation cost of $2.50/W is below the national median of ~$2.70/W.
