Local Context: Candler County, GA

Electricity rates: Candler County pays 14.74¢/kWh, which is 11% below the U.S. weighted-average rate of 16.5¢/kWh (EIA 2025).

Solar resource: With approximately 3.7 peak sun hours per day, a 6kW system in Candler County pays back in 11.0 years — rank #44 of 159 in GA. This is roughly in line with the GA average of 11.2 years.

Range within GA: Payback across 159 counties ranges from 10.6 years (fastest) to 13.5 years (slowest) — a spread of 2.9 years. Candler County sits above the state median in solar attractiveness.

Net metering impact: Net metering rules vary by utility within GA. Confirm your specific provider's export rate before sizing the system. See the full GA guide for policy details.

Bottom line: Candler County has moderate solar economics. Payback of 11.0 years is reasonable but sensitive to installation cost and local incentives. The estimates above assume a $2.70/W + $1,500 fixed cost install, 30% ITC, and year-13 inverter replacement — see methodology.
