Solar Panel Cost Breakdown in 2026: What You Actually Pay For

When you receive a solar quote for $20,000, it's easy to assume most of that money goes to the panels. In reality, hardware represents less than 40% of the total installed cost. Here's where the money actually goes in 2026.

Typical 6kW system cost breakdown

  • Solar panels (12-16 panels): $4,000-$5,500 (25-30% of total)
  • Inverter (string or micro): $1,500-$3,000 (8-15%)
  • Mounting & racking: $1,000-$1,500 (5-8%)
  • Electrical (wiring, conduit, breakers): $800-$1,500 (4-8%)
  • Labor & installation: $2,500-$4,000 (12-20%)
  • Permitting & interconnection: $500-$2,000 (3-10%)
  • Sales, design, and installer margin: $3,000-$6,000 (15-30%)

Total: roughly $2.50-$3.20 per watt before the 30% ITC, depending on state and installer.

Why state-by-state costs vary so much

California installs average $2.85/W, while Texas averages $2.40/W. The difference comes from:

  • Permitting complexity — California requires more inspections and paperwork
  • Labor costs — union labor in CA vs non-union in TX
  • Utility interconnection fees — CA utilities charge more
  • Competition — more installers in mature markets drives prices down

How to reduce cost without sacrificing quality

Three proven levers:

  1. Get 3-5 quotes. Installer margins vary 100%+ within the same market. The cheapest quote is often $4,000-$6,000 less than the most expensive.
  2. Avoid the door-to-door sales channel. Installers that advertise through canvassing have 15-25% higher prices to fund sales commissions. Local installers and EnergySage referrals are typically cheaper.
  3. Size to usage, not maximal. Oversizing adds cost but produces generation you can't fully offset (especially in NEM 3.0 states). The most common over-sizing mistake wastes $3,000-$5,000.

What not to cut

Don't save $500 by hiring a non-certified installer — it can void panel warranties and complicate insurance. Don't skip the monitoring system ($200-$400) — catching a 5% production drop early pays for itself many times over.

Bottom line

Expect $2.50-$3.20/W installed in 2026. After 30% ITC, net cost is $1.75-$2.25/W — meaning a 6kW system nets at $10,500-$13,500. Our calculator uses $2.70/W + $1,500 fixed cost as the national median.
