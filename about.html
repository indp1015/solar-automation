Methodology & Data Sources

⚡

Electricity Rates

Sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) 2025 state averages. We use the residential sector (RES) annual data.

☀️

Solar Production

Generated via PVGIS v5.2 (European Commission JRC) utilizing NSRDB (NREL) data. Note: US radiation data is based on a 2005-2015 average.

🏛️

Incentives

Referenced from DSIRE (CC-BY-SA 4.0). Includes federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) and state-level policies.

Our ROI calculations use a 25-year projection, accounting for a 0.5% annual panel degradation and a 2.5% annual electricity rate increase. The default system cost is $2.70 per watt before the 30% ITC.
