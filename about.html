About Solar ROI Calculator

Our mission is to provide transparent, data-driven estimates for solar panel investments across the United States.

Methodology & Data Sources

  • Electricity Rates: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) 2025 state averages.
  • Solar Production: PVGIS v5.2 (European Commission JRC) using NSRDB (NREL) data. Note: US radiation data is based on a 2005-2015 average.
  • Incentives: Referenced from DSIRE (CC-BY-SA 4.0).

Our ROI calculations use a 25-year projection, accounting for a 0.5% annual panel degradation and a 2.5% annual electricity rate increase.
