Net Metering: No statewide net metering

Texas has no statewide net metering mandate. Some retail electric providers (REPs) offer buyback programs, but compensation varies widely — typically 3–8¢/kWh for exported solar, well below the ~15¢ retail rate. Self-consumption is critical to ROI.

Key Incentives in Texas

  • Property Tax Exemption — 100% of appraised solar value is exempt from property tax
  • Federal ITC 30% through 2032
  • No state income tax, so state-level tax credits do not apply

Major Utilities

Oncor, CenterPoint, AEP Texas (TDU varies by county). Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 15.40¢/kWh and ~14,000 kWh of annual usage, Texas homeowners typically see payback periods between 7.6 years (in Hudspeth County) and 9.9 years (in Montgomery County). Installation cost of $2.55/W is below the national median of ~$2.70/W.
