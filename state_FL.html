Net Metering: 1:1 Net Metering

Florida mandates 1:1 retail credit for exported solar through investor-owned utilities (FPL, Duke Energy Florida, TECO, Gulf Power). Excess generation is credited at the full retail rate, making self-consumption and export equally valuable.

Key Incentives in Florida

  • Property Tax Exemption — 100% of solar value exempt from property tax
  • Sales Tax Exemption — solar equipment exempt from Florida sales tax
  • Federal ITC 30% through 2032

Major Utilities

FPL, Duke Energy Florida, TECO, Gulf Power. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 15.21¢/kWh and ~12,000 kWh of annual usage, Florida homeowners typically see payback periods between 9.5 years (in Lee County) and 10.8 years (in Liberty County). Installation cost of $2.40/W is below the national median of ~$2.70/W.
