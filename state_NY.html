Net Metering: VDER (Value of Distributed Energy Resources)

New York's VDER program replaced traditional net metering for new systems in most utility territories. Export compensation is based on time and location value, typically 60–90% of retail for residential systems in NYC, less upstate.

Key Incentives in New York

  • NY-Sun Incentive — upfront $/W rebate based on region and income
  • State Tax Credit — 25% of system cost up to $5,000
  • Property Tax Exemption — 15-year real property tax abatement

Major Utilities

Con Edison, National Grid, NYSEG, PSEG Long Island. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 26.39¢/kWh and ~7,000 kWh of annual usage, New York homeowners typically see payback periods between 6.3 years (in Nassau County) and 7.4 years (in Franklin County). Installation cost of $3.00/W is above the national median of ~$2.70/W.
