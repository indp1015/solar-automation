Net Metering: CGS+ (Customer Grid-Supply Plus)

Hawaii replaced traditional net metering with CGS+ in 2015. Export compensation is roughly 50% of retail — currently around 15–20¢/kWh vs a 40¢ retail rate. Hawaii's extremely high electricity rates still make solar highly attractive despite lower export rates.

Key Incentives in Hawaii

  • State Tax Credit — 35% of system cost up to $5,000
  • Property Tax Exemption — varies by county
  • Federal ITC 30% through 2032

Major Utilities

Hawaiian Electric (HECO), Maui Electric, Hawaii Electric Light. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 40.40¢/kWh and ~6,000 kWh of annual usage, Hawaii homeowners typically see payback periods between 4.6 years (in Honolulu County) and 5.4 years (in Kauai County). Installation cost of $3.20/W is above the national median of ~$2.70/W.
