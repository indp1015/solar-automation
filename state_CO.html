Net Metering: 1:1 Net Metering

Colorado mandates 1:1 retail credit for residential solar under 120% of annual usage. Xcel Energy, the state's largest utility, offers net metering plus a small renewable energy credit (REC) payment for exported generation.

Key Incentives in Colorado

  • Property Tax Exemption — 100% of residential solar value exempt
  • Sales Tax Exemption — solar equipment exempt from state sales tax
  • Federal ITC 30% through 2032

Major Utilities

Xcel Energy, Black Hills Energy, Colorado Springs Utilities. Net metering rules may vary by utility even within the same state — confirm with your local provider before signing a contract.

What This Means for ROI

At 15.69¢/kWh and ~7,000 kWh of annual usage, Colorado homeowners typically see payback periods between 8.9 years (in Alamosa County) and 11.1 years (in San Juan County). Installation cost of $2.65/W is below the national median of ~$2.70/W.
